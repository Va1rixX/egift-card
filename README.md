## 🛠️ Configuration
After installing the application, you’ll need to configure it:

1. **Open the App**: Launch **egift-card** from your applications list.
2. **Set Up Your Account**: Enter your email and business details.
3. **Choose a Template**: Select a template from the library or create a new one.
4. **Customize Your Message**: Tailor the message for your customers.
5. **Save Your Changes**: Don’t forget to save your template.

## 📧 Sending E-Gift Cards
Once everything is set up, sending e-gift cards is straightforward:

1. **Select Template**: Choose a pre-designed template that fits your needs.
2. **Enter Recipient's Information**: Fill in details like the recipient's name and email.
3. **Personalize Your Gift Card**: Add a personalized message.
4. **Preview Your E-Gift Card**: Make sure everything looks perfect.
5. **Send**: Click the send button to deliver your e-gift card.
